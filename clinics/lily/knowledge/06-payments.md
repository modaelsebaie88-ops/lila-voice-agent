# Fees, payment, and insurance
<!-- DEMO VALUES – confirm with the clinic before going live. -->

## Consultation fees
<!-- DEMO PRICES – invented for the demo. Replace with the clinic's real fees. -->
Without insurance, a general consultation costs 120 dollars, a routine checkup costs 150 dollars, and a follow-up visit costs 80 dollars. Prices are the same with either doctor. With insurance, you usually pay only your plan's copay.

## Insurance companies accepted
<!-- DEMO LIST – replace with the plans the clinic really accepts. -->
The clinic accepts Aetna, Blue Cross Blue Shield, Cigna, UnitedHealthcare, and Medicare. If your insurance isn't on this list, the clinic team can check whether your plan is covered before your visit, or you can pay the standard fee.

## Using your insurance
Please bring your insurance card to every visit. Some insurance plans need approval before certain tests or treatments; the clinic team can help you with the paperwork.

## How to pay
You pay at the reception desk after your appointment. The clinic accepts cash and debit or credit cards.

## Receipts
You'll get a receipt for every payment. If you need a detailed receipt for your insurance or employer, ask at reception or request one from the clinic team.

## Questions about a bill
If you have a question about a bill or think a charge is wrong, the receptionist can take a message, and the clinic's billing team will look into it and call you back.
