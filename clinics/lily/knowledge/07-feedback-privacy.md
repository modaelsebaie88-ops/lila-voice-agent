# Feedback, complaints, and privacy
<!-- DEMO VALUES – confirm with the clinic before going live. -->

## Making a complaint
The clinic takes every complaint seriously. The receptionist can take the details, and the clinic manager will review it and call you back within 2 working days.

## Compliments and suggestions
Compliments and suggestions are always welcome. The receptionist can pass them on to the clinic team.

## Privacy and confidentiality
Everything you tell the clinic is confidential. Your health information is only shared with the people caring for you, or with your permission. The clinic never discusses a patient's details with someone else unless the patient has agreed.

## Speaking for someone else
A relative or carer can call to book an appointment for a patient. To discuss the patient's medical information or existing appointments, the patient needs to give the clinic their permission first.
