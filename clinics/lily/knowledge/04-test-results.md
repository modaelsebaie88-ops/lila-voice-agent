## How you get your results
A doctor checks every test result first. If anything needs to be discussed, the clinic team will contact you. For privacy, results are never given out by the virtual receptionist, and only to the patient themselves or someone they have authorised.

## How long results take
Most routine blood test results are ready within 3 to 5 working days. Some specialist tests and scans take longer.

## Asking about your results
If you are waiting for a result, or want the doctor to explain one, the receptionist can take a message, and the clinic team will call you back. If the doctor wants to discuss a result in person, you'll be offered an appointment.

## Preparing for a blood test
Some blood tests need you to fast first. The doctor or the clinic team will tell you if you need to fast and for how long when the test is arranged. If you aren't sure, ask the clinic team before the test.
