## Repeat prescriptions
Patients who already take a medicine prescribed by a doctor at the clinic can ask for a repeat prescription. The receptionist takes the request: the patient's name, the medicine's name and dose as written on the label, and the pharmacy they use. A doctor reviews every request before it is approved.

## How long a repeat prescription takes
Please allow 2 working days for a repeat prescription. Ask before you run out. The clinic team will call you if the doctor needs to see you first or has a question.

## When an appointment is needed
The doctor may ask to see you before renewing a medicine, for example if you haven't had a review for a while or if the medicine needs regular checks. A new medicine always needs an appointment with a doctor.

## Running out of medicine today
If you have run out of an important medicine, tell the receptionist, and your request will be marked as urgent for the clinic team. If you feel unwell because you have missed your medicine, seek urgent medical help.

## Questions about side effects or how to take a medicine
The receptionist cannot give advice about medicines. For questions about side effects or how to take a medicine, speak to your pharmacist, or book an appointment or ask for a callback from the clinic team.
