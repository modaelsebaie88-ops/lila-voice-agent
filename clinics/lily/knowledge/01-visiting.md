## Location and directions
<!-- DEMO ADDRESS – invented for the demo. Replace with the clinic's real address. -->
Lily's Clinic is at 120 Garden Street, Suite 200, Springfield, on the second floor of the Garden Medical Building, across the street from the public library. The entrance is on Garden Street, next to the pharmacy, and the clinic is signposted from the lobby.

## Parking and public transport
There is free parking for patients in the lot behind the building; enter from Elm Lane. The number 12 bus stops on Garden Street, right outside the building, and taxis can drop off at the main entrance.

## Accessibility
The clinic is fully accessible. There is a step-free entrance, an elevator to the second floor, and a wheelchair-accessible restroom. A wheelchair is available at reception, and the team is happy to help anyone who needs assistance; just let the receptionist know when you book.

## What to bring to your appointment
Please bring a photo ID, your insurance card if you have one, a list of all the medicines you take (including vitamins and supplements, with their doses), and any recent test results, scans, or letters from other doctors. It also helps to write down your questions for the doctor before you come.

## Arriving and checking in
Please arrive 10 minutes before your appointment time to check in. On your first visit, please arrive 15 minutes early so the team can register you and take your details.

## Running late
If you're running late, please call the clinic as soon as you can. You can still be seen if you arrive within 15 minutes of your appointment time. If you are more than 15 minutes late, the doctor may not be able to see you, and the team will offer you the next free time that day or rebook you.

## Bringing someone with you
You are welcome to bring a family member or friend with you to your appointment. Children under 18 must come with a parent or legal guardian.

## Languages spoken
The clinic team and doctors speak Arabic and English. Patients can call or visit in either language.

## Masks and infections
If you have a fever, cough, or rash that might be contagious, please tell the team when you arrive so they can seat you away from other patients.
