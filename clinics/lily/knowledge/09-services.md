## Services the clinic offers
Lily's Clinic is a general practice and family medicine clinic for patients of all ages, with Dr. Amira Hassan and Dr. James Carter. You can book a general consultation for any new health problem, a routine checkup when nothing specific is wrong, or a follow-up visit about something a doctor here has already seen you for. The clinic can also renew repeat prescriptions, give sick notes and medical certificates after an assessment, refer you to a specialist when a doctor thinks you need one, and help with medical records and forms.

## Vaccinations
The clinic gives the seasonal flu vaccine, tetanus boosters, hepatitis B vaccines, and the routine childhood vaccines. Vaccinations need an appointment: book a routine checkup and mention the vaccine you need, so the team can have it ready.

## Lab tests and blood tests at the clinic
Blood can be taken at the clinic, Monday to Friday from 9 in the morning to 1 in the afternoon, when a doctor has asked for a test. Most results are ready within 3 to 5 working days, and a doctor checks them before the clinic team contacts you.

## Online or phone consultations
Video consultations are available for follow-up visits and for questions that don't need an examination. Book a follow-up visit and tell the receptionist you'd like it by video; the clinic team will send you a link before the appointment. A first visit or a new problem needs an appointment in person.

## Home visits
The clinic doesn't offer home visits. If a patient can't leave home, the clinic team can arrange a video consultation instead, and in an emergency you should call emergency services.
