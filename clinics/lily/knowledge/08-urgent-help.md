# Urgent help and outside opening hours
<!-- DEMO VALUES – confirm with the clinic before going live. -->

## When the clinic is closed
Outside opening hours, the virtual receptionist can still book, change, or cancel appointments and take messages. The clinic team reads messages and calls back when the clinic reopens.

## Medical emergencies
In an emergency, such as chest pain, severe difficulty breathing, heavy bleeding, signs of a stroke, a severe allergic reaction, or loss of consciousness, call emergency services straight away. Do not wait for a clinic appointment.

## Urgent but not an emergency
If you need to see a doctor soon but it isn't an emergency, the receptionist can look for the earliest free appointment, including today. If nothing is free soon enough, an urgent care centre or emergency department can help.

## Mental health crisis
If you are thinking about harming yourself or feel unable to keep yourself safe, please call the crisis line or emergency services now. You don't have to go through this alone.
