# Medical records, referrals, and letters
<!-- DEMO VALUES – confirm with the clinic before going live. -->

## Getting a copy of your medical records
Patients can ask for a copy of their medical records. The receptionist takes the request, and the clinic team will call to confirm your identity and how you'd like to receive them. You'll need photo ID to collect them. Requests are usually ready within 10 working days.

## Sending records to another doctor or hospital
The clinic can send your records to another doctor or hospital with your permission. The receptionist takes the request with the name of the doctor or hospital, and the clinic team will arrange it.

## Referrals to a specialist
Referrals are decided by a doctor during an appointment. If a doctor has already agreed to refer you and you'd like to know how it is going, the receptionist can take a message, and the clinic team will call you with an update.

## Sick notes and medical certificates
A sick note or medical certificate needs a doctor's assessment, so for a current illness please book an appointment. If you need a copy of a note from an earlier visit, the receptionist can take a request for the clinic team.

## Forms and letters
For forms or letters, such as for work, school, travel, sports, or insurance, the receptionist can take a request. Some forms need an appointment with a doctor, and some may have a fee. The clinic team will call you to let you know.
