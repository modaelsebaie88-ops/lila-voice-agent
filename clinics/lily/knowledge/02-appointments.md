# Appointments

## Types of appointment
A general consultation is for a new health problem or anything you'd like a doctor to look at, and takes about 20 minutes. A routine checkup is a preventive visit when nothing specific is wrong, and takes about 30 minutes. A follow-up visit continues care for something a doctor at the clinic has already seen you about, and takes about 15 minutes.

## Same-day appointments
Same-day appointments are offered when a doctor has a free time that day. The receptionist can check today's free times on the call.

## Walk-ins
Booking an appointment is recommended. Walk-in patients are seen only if a doctor has a free time, so the wait can be long.

## Cancelling or changing an appointment
Please cancel or change your appointment at least 24 hours before it, so another patient can have the time. You can cancel or change it on the phone with the receptionist, using your booking reference or the phone number you booked with.

## Missed appointments
If you can't come, please let the clinic know, even at short notice. If a patient misses several appointments without telling the clinic, the team may ask them to call to book future visits.

## Waiting list for an earlier time
If there is no suitable time, the receptionist can put you on the waiting list. If an earlier time becomes free because someone cancels, the clinic team will call you to offer it.

## Booking for a family member
You can book an appointment for your child, partner, or a relative. The receptionist needs the patient's own full name and a phone number to reach them or you. For privacy, details of someone else's existing appointment can only be given to the person who booked it or the patient.

## Children and teenagers
The clinic sees patients of all ages. Children under 18 must come with a parent or legal guardian, who will be asked to consent to their care.

## Booking reference
Every booking gets a six-digit booking reference. Keep it to change or cancel the appointment quickly.

## Seeing the same doctor
Returning patients can ask to see the same doctor again, and the receptionist will look for that doctor's free times.
